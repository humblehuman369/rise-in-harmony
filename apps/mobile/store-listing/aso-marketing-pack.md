## 3) App Preview script (~20 seconds)

**Format:** 15–30s vertical (9:16), 1080×1920 or device native.  
**Audio:** In-app tone (soft 528 Hz) under VO, or VO-only with UI SFX.  
**VO tone:** Calm, warm, unhurried — not “hype ad.”

### Shot list + VO

| Time | On screen | Voiceover (optional) | Captions burned-in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:03** | App icon → splash / home | “Mornings don’t have to startle you.” | *Wake gently* |
| **0:03–0:07** | Player: 528 Hz playing, rings animate | “Rise In Harmony plays precise healing frequencies.” | *TrueHz™ precision* |
| **0:07–0:11** | Alarm screen: set time + frequency | “Set a soft frequency alarm.” | *Gentle alarm* |
| **0:11–0:16** | Meditate: TrueHz session card → play | “Sink into TrueHz meditations.” | *6 TrueHz sessions* |
| **0:16–0:19** | Sound Studio layers or library grid | “Layer soundscapes. Explore Solfeggio.” | *Your sound ritual* |
| **0:19–0:22** | Logo + name end card | “Rise In Harmony.” | *Download free* |

**Total target:** ~20–22 seconds (Apple allows up to 30s).

### Full VO script (spoken, ~20s at calm pace)

> Mornings don’t have to startle you.  
> Rise In Harmony plays precise TrueHz frequencies —  
> set a gentle alarm, explore Solfeggio tones,  
> and sink into TrueHz meditations.  
> Begin every day in harmony.

### No-VO version (music-led)
1. Soft 528 Hz bed throughout  
2. Captions only (from table above)  
3. End card: logo + “Rise In Harmony” + “Free on the App Store”

### Production tips
- Record on a real device or high-quality simulator capture  
- 60 fps if possible; avoid busy UI transitions  
- Keep first **3 seconds** crystal clear (autoplay is muted often—captions essential)  
- Export: H.264 or ProRes → upload in ASC as App Preview for iPhone **6.9"** (1320×2868)
- **Ready-to-upload file:** `app-preview/app-preview-20s-69inch.mp4` (1320×2868, H.264, 30fps, ~21s)  
- Do **not** show medical claims, competitor apps, or pricing that can go stale
